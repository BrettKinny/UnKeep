# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in UnKeep welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical choices.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- being respectful, patient, and specific;
- assuming good intent while taking impact seriously;
- giving and receiving actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and repairing harm;
- respecting privacy and coordinated vulnerability disclosure; and
- keeping disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate exposure of private
information, impersonation, or encouraging others to engage in such conduct.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project-managed communication spaces, and public situations where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the lead maintainer through the contact
details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/BrettKinny). Do not post
sensitive incident details publicly. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept
confidential to the extent practical. A maintainer implicated in a report will
not adjudicate that report when another suitable reviewer is available.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, impose a
temporary restriction, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces.
Decisions will consider context, impact, patterns of behavior, and community
safety.
